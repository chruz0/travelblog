# Paradisiac — Travel Blog

**[Live Site](https://chruz0.github.io/travelblog/)**

A themeable travel blog exploring a focused research question: how does the treatment and captivity of dolphins at a popular Jamaican attraction affect a visitor's experience, and what should travelers weigh before deciding to participate? Rather than a typical destination write-up, the site combines firsthand field observations with secondary research to give readers a balanced, source-backed picture before they visit.

Originally created as a multimodal research project for a college composition course.

## Features

- **Theme switcher** — five selectable color themes (Teal, Coral, Sand, Ocean Blue, Sunset Orange) plus a Night Mode toggle, built in vanilla JavaScript as an accessibility-minded design choice for reading comfort and contrast preference
- **Long-form guide format** — a TL;DR summary, firsthand field notes, cited ethical and environmental analysis, and a full Works Cited section (7 sources)
- **Panel-based structure** — content organized into Field Notes, Promises, Ethical Concerns, Mitigation Measures, and Alternatives, guiding readers from lived experience to evidence to their own decision
- **Responsive layout** built with HTML5 and CSS3, no frameworks

## Research approach

- **Primary research:** firsthand observations from an in-person visit, including guest experience, trainer behavior, and dolphin responses
- **Secondary research:** news coverage, NEPA environmental filings, and marine-mammal welfare studies, linked directly so readers can verify claims themselves

## Tech stack

- HTML5
- CSS3
- Vanilla JavaScript (theme switching)
- Hosted via GitHub Pages
